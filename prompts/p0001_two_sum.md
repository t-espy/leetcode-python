## Contract

`two_sum(nums: list[int], target: int) -> list[int]`

`nums` holds at least two integers. Return the two distinct indices `i` and
`j` such that `nums[i] + nums[j] == target`. Exactly one such pair exists.
Order of the two indices does not matter. Each index is used at most once.
